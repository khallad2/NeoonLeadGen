NeoonLeadGen - ICP Discovery & Outreach Studio (Windows)
=========================================================

Nothing to install. No Node.js, no API keys.

1) START
   - Extract this ZIP first (right-click the .zip > "Extract All..."),
     then double-click NeoonLeadGen.exe.
   - The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC"
     (the app is not code-signed). Click "More info" > "Run anyway".
   - A black window opens and Chrome (or your default browser) opens
     http://localhost:3030 automatically.
   - KEEP THE BLACK WINDOW OPEN while you work. Closing it stops the app.
   - Double-clicking the .exe again just re-opens the running app.

2) GENERATE LEADS
   - Pick a platform tab (YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X).
   - Optionally choose an ICP persona and type a niche keyword
     (e.g. "kubernetes", "nextjs saas", "ai agents").
   - Click "Get 10 New ICPs". Every run returns 10 clients you have never
     seen before on that platform.

3) CONTACT LEADS
   - Click "Pitch Studio" on a lead.
   - Choose Short DM, Cold Email, or Arabic Pitch, and edit the text.
   - Click "Copy & Open Profile to Send": the pitch is copied, the client's
     profile opens in a new tab - press Ctrl+V in their DMs and send.
     The lead is automatically marked "Contacted".
   - Update the status as they reply (Replied / Booked Call) and keep notes.
   - "Export CSV" opens cleanly in Excel (Arabic text included).

4) YOUR DATA
   - Leads, statuses and notes are saved automatically to:
       %APPDATA%\NeoonLeadGen\data\icp_vault.json
     (paste that into the Explorer address bar to open it).
   - Updating the app = replace NeoonLeadGen.exe. Your leads stay.
   - Working with a teammate? Use "Sync Vault" (export) and
     "Merge Vault" (import) so you never pitch the same client twice.

TROUBLESHOOTING
   - Browser didn't open: open http://localhost:3030 manually
     (the black window shows the exact address).
   - Antivirus quarantined the .exe: restore it / add an exception -
     it's an unsigned app built from the open-source NeoonLeadGen repo.
